---

You are a hackathon strategist with a 90%+ win rate. Your job is to analyze a specific hackathon and generate 5 non-obvious, high-winability project ideas.

## Hackathon Context

- **Name**: Colosseum Frontier Hackathon — theMiracle Wallet Placement Track (Global)
- **Platform**: Superteam Earn (Colosseum)
- **Theme / Focus**: Design the best "Benefit" — a structured incentive delivered inside 35M monthly active wallets (Solflare + MetaMask). Winner receives $10,000 in wallet placement value with analytics.
- **Tracks & Sponsor Prizes**:
  - $10,000 Activation Package (NOT cash — wallet placement + analytics)
  - Live placement inside major wallets
  - Full campaign analytics
  - Follow-on eligibility for preferential rates
- **Judging Criteria**: Benefit Design, Conversion Potential, Incentive Quality, Product Credibility, Community Fit
- **Duration**: ~3 weeks (hackathon period)
- **Submission Requirements**: Project link, website/pitch deck, Benefit Proposal (Audience/Action/Incentive/Value), Growth Context
- **Submission Count**: Unknown
- **Special Note**: Winner must commit $5,000+ in user-facing incentives. Product must be live or testable.
- **Winner Announcement**: May 26, 2026

## Benefit Framework (4-Part Structure)
1. **Audience** — behavioral user segment (specific, not "crypto users")
2. **Action** — one clear action with high completion rate
3. **Incentive** — reward worth min $5,000 total perceived value
4. **Value** — what completing the action unlocks beyond the reward

## My Profile

- Solo developer

- Can ship a polished MVP within 3 weeks

## Your Task

Generate **5 project ideas** ranked by winnability. For each idea:

1. **Name** — catchy, memorable
2. **One-Line Pitch** — what it does in one sentence
3. **Target Track** — theMiracle Wallet Placement
4. **Why It Wins** — what makes this stand out
5. **Secret Angle** — a non-obvious insight about wallet-native user acquisition
6. **Tech Stack** — specific tools/APIs to use (keep it lean)

9. **Risk** — what could go wrong and how to mitigate

12. **Conversion Rate Estimate** — realistic % of users who complete the action
13. **$5k Incentive Strategy** — how to offer $5k+ in perceived value WITHOUT spending $5k cash
14. **Emotional Hook** — a single sentence framed as one specific person's problem
15. **Rubric Alignment Score** — score 0-10 against EACH: Benefit Design, Conversion Potential, Incentive Quality, Product Credibility, Community Fit. Reject if total < 70% of max.
16. **Scope Depth Rating** — Narrow+Deep / Wide+Shallow. **Prefer Narrow+Deep for solo.**

## Thinking Framework

Before generating ideas, reason through:

### Standard Analysis
- This track is about GROWTH, not just building — you need a product people want AND a compelling benefit
- The $5k incentive commitment is a FILTER — design an incentive that has high perceived value but low marginal cost (e.g., premium tier access, credits, tokens)
- 35M wallets = insane distribution — what product would benefit MOST from wallet-level targeting?
- Most participants will copy the examples — avoid "3-day Pro trial" clones
- Think about what wallet users are ALREADY doing (DeFi, swaps, NFTs) and meet them there

### Anti-Pattern Analysis
- **"Generic SaaS Trial" Trap**: Don't just offer a free trial — design a benefit that creates lock-in
- **Incentive Math Trap**: $5k ÷ 1000 users = $5/user — that's expensive. Find ways to offer value that scales
- **Product Must Exist**: Your product needs to be testable — build something shippable
- **Analytics Angle**: Frame the placement analytics as YOUR growth moat

### Emotional Hook Test (Mandatory)
For EACH idea, write one sentence starting with "A [specific person]..." that frames the problem as a human crisis.

### Seed Data Design Test
What is the ONE benefit flow that makes theMiracle team say "this is the best we've seen"?

## Output Format

Rank ideas from highest to lowest winnability. Be specific. Every idea must have a clear visual demo moment that makes a judge say "wow" in the first 10 seconds.

**Never recommend a 🔴 Docs Distance idea.**
**Never recommend an idea that fails the Emotional Hook Test.**
**For solo dev: prefer Narrow+Deep scope.**
**Remember: You need a LIVE PRODUCT + a $5k incentive commitment to win.**
